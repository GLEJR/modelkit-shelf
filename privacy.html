Privacy policy

ModelKit Companion, by Liminal Interactive. Last updated 17 November 2026.

The short version: your collection is yours. The app works fully on your phone without an account. If you make one, we keep a copy of your collection so it's backed up and syncs between your devices. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data, ever.

What stays on your phone

  • Everything you add: kits, build stages, timer sessions and progress photos are saved on your phone first.
  • Barcode scanning happens on your phone. The camera picture never leaves it.
  • Share cards are drawn on your phone and handed to the app you choose. We don't upload them.
  • Backups you export are files you save wherever you pick. We never see them.

What we keep if you make an account

  • Your sign-in: your email address, and your password if you set one (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Your collection: the kits, stages, sessions and photos you add, so they sync and are backed up. Only you can read them.
  • Your shelf, only if you switch it on: the kits you pick, their photos, stage and hours, published at a link only you hand out. Switching the shelf off removes it.
  • The shared kit catalogue: when you add a kit by barcode, its facts (barcode, name, brand, line, scale, type) go into a shared catalogue so other people can scan the same box. Nothing personal goes in.

We store this with Supabase, our hosting provider. It's encrypted in transit, and access rules on the server stop anyone reading anyone else's collection.

Other services the app talks to

  • UPCitemdb: if a barcode isn't in our catalogue yet, the app asks UPCitemdb what it is. They receive the barcode and, like any website, your phone's internet address. See their privacy policy.
  • Google ML Kit: the barcode scanner is Google's, running on your phone. Google says it sends Google basic diagnostics (things like the device model, app version and error codes, with an identifier that isn't meant to identify you or your device), not your camera picture. See Google's disclosure.
  • Email: sign-in links and password resets are sent by email to the address you give.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no data brokers.
  • We don't sell or rent your data, or use it to profile you.
  • Your email isn't shown to anyone, on the shelf or anywhere else.

Deleting things

  • Things you delete go to Recently deleted for 30 days so you can undo, then they're removed for good, from your phone and our server, photo files included.
  • Delete my account (in the app, under Account) removes your sign-in, your whole collection and photos on our server, and your shelf, straight away. It offers to export a backup first. Kits you added to the shared catalogue stay there (they're facts about a box, not about you) but are no longer linked to you.
  • Signing out removes nothing; your kits stay on your phone.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the app will point you to it before anything new applies to your data. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/GLEJR/modelkit-shelf/issues (it's public, so leave out personal details and ask for a private reply).
